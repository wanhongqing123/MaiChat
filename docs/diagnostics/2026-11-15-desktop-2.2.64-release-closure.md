passed 68/0/0; focused Panel passed 12/0/0; twelve Core suites and console checks
all exited zero. Same-source Mac Core/Runner, focused Bridge 6/0 and Panel 7/0 passed.
Draft preservation, background Session isolation, switch/back, Stop and late start
after completion were covered. This is not a complete two-account network run.

The video report was a new actual 2.2.63 report. Native window geometry was already
final while retained Metal/parent presentation geometry remained at the old size.
The final GUI-layer synchronization passed 65 mapped samples without mismatch;
Mac's affected normal group passed 16/0/0. Its continuous compositor recording
boundary is retained. The ordinary window policy now caps large sources at
960x540 logical and 75% of available area, subject to minimum control dimensions.
Windows' original three generated source sizes produced 640x360/960x540/960x540
logical ordinary windows, with one visible native size per case. The amended
original three-row Qt selector passed 5/0/0 including init/cleanup.
